1. Problem Statement

Railway passengers face safety risks while entering and exiting trains. The gap between the train and platform, crowded platforms and accidental falls can cause serious problems. Traditional platforms do not always provide automatic protection.

2. Our Innovative Solution

We propose an Automatic Railway Platform Safety Barrier System with an extendable and retractable movable walkway. It connects the train and platform after the train stops. Sensors control the system and help passengers move safely.

An additional feature converts some of the mechanical energy from safety-door movement into electrical energy.

3. Smart Sensors

Train Position Sensor

Checks whether the train has stopped at the correct position.

Proximity Sensor

Checks the distance and alignment between the train and platform.

Obstacle Sensor

Detects objects or passengers in the walkway and door movement area.

Pressure Sensor

Can detect passenger presence on the movable walkway.

Door Position Sensor

Confirms whether the safety doors are completely open or closed.

5. Complete Working Process

Step 1 - Train Arrival

The train arrives at the railway platform.

Step 2 - Position Detection

Sensors confirm that the train has stopped at the correct position.

Step 3 - Safety Doors Open

Automatic platform safety barriers open after the train is secured.

Step 4 - Movable Walkway Extends

A movable walkway extends between the train and platform to bridge the gap.

Step 5 - Passenger Movement

Passengers can safely enter and exit the train using the walkway.

Step 6 - Walkway Retracts

After the passenger area is clear, the movable walkway moves back into its housing.

Step 7 - Safety Doors Close

The sensors confirm that the walkway is retracted and the area is clear. The safety barriers close.

Step 8 - Train Departure

The system confirms that all barriers are closed and the train can depart after railway safety checks.

6. Electricity Generation Mechanism

Our additional feature uses the movement of the automatic safety doors to generate electricity.

Mechanical Energy Conversion

Step 1: Door Movement

When the automatic safety door opens or closes, its linear movement drives a connected rack mechanism.

Step 2: Rack and Pinion Gear

Rack
→
⚙️
Pinion Gear

The rack moves in a straight line. Its teeth engage with the pinion gear, converting linear movement into rotational movement.

Step 3: One-Way Clutch

A one-way clutch helps transfer rotation to the generator in the required direction during door movement.

Step 4: Electric Generator

Rotating Shaft
→
N S
Generator

The rotating shaft drives the generator. Inside the generator, relative movement between magnets and coils induces electrical voltage through electromagnetic induction.

Step 5: Rectifier

The electrical output is passed through a rectifier circuit to obtain DC electricity suitable for storage.

Step 6: Energy Storage

The DC output can be stored in a rechargeable battery or capacitor through a suitable charging and protection circuit.

Step 7: Electricity Usage

The stored energy can support low-power applications such as indicator lights, small sensors or monitoring displays.

Our proposed RAILSAFE system aims to make railway platforms safer and smarter. By combining automatic safety barriers, a movable walkway, smart sensors and mechanical energy recovery, we aim to improve passenger safety and introduce an innovative railway solution.
